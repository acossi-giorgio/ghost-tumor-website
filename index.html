Scientific Concept

Why 'Ghost Tumor'? Breaching Cancer's Fortress

Every solid tumor is embedded in a dense protein scaffold - the Extracellular Matrix (ECM) - which governs cell proliferation, tissue invasion, and therapeutic resistance. Colorectal cancer cells remodel this scaffold, creating a dense physical and biochemical barrier that excludes immune cell infiltration.

CONCEPT 01

The 'Ghost' Scaffold (dECM)

Through gentle chemical and enzymatic detergent wash cycles, every cell is cleared from patient colorectal resections. The result is a translucent, cell-free biological scaffold retaining native architecture, collagen composition, and microenvironmental cues.

CONCEPT 02

3D Recellularization

The freeze-dried dECM is integrated into photocrosslinkable GelMA hydrogels and repopulated with human colorectal adenocarcinoma cells and stromal fibroblasts, reconstituting genuine 3D tissue architecture in vitro.

System Components

From 2D Monolayer to 3D GelMA Bioprinting

To overcome the limitations of 2D plastic plates and alginate's failure to support stromal cells long-term, the platform integrates four synergistic biological components:

EpCAM+ Tumor

LoVo Cells (Tumor)

Human colorectal adenocarcinoma line (EpCAM+). In the 3D biomaterial, cells exhibit high viability and organize spontaneously into tight multicellular tumor clusters.

Vimentin+ Stroma

CAFs (Stromal Fibroblasts)

Patient-derived Cancer-Associated Fibroblasts (Vimentin+). They remodel ECM and provide biomechanical cues. GelMA provides essential RGD adhesion motifs for their anchorage.

Patient 5 mg/mL

Patient-Derived dECM

Decellularized extracellular matrix lyophilized and milled into powder (5 mg/mL), enriching the bioink with patient-specific biochemical signals.

Immunity Dynamic

PBMC & Bioreactor (Ongoing)

Autologous peripheral blood lymphocytes under dynamic perfusion in bioreactors to assess immune infiltration and personalized immunotherapy response.

Rationale: Transition from Alginate to GelMA

During exploratory phases, alginate hydrogels resulted in declining metabolic rates and limited survival for Cancer-Associated Fibroblasts due to a lack of intrinsic cell attachment motifs. Switching to Gelatin Methacryloyl (GelMA), a collagen-derived biomaterial with native RGD integrin-binding sequences, restored fibroblast spreading and long-term stromal viability.

Standard Operating Procedure

From Resection to 3D Micro-Constructs in 8 Steps

The standardized protocol illustrated on our exhibition poster, ensuring reproducible and uniform 3D cancer replicates for drug screening:

01 LYOPHIL

1. Lyophilization

GelMA and patient dECM are dehydrated using a vacuum freeze-dryer (Heto LyoPro 3000) to yield stable, porous matrix sponges ready for reconstitution.

02 BIOINK

2. Bioink Formulation

Lyophilized GelMA is dissolved in sterile saline alongside biocompatible photoinitiators (Riboflavin + Sodium Persulfate) to create the liquid bioink.

03 HARVEST

3. Cell Detachment & Centrifugation

LoVo adenocarcinoma cells and CAFs are harvested from culture flasks under laminar flow hoods and concentrated into distinct cell pellets.

04 COUNT

4. Cell Counting & Density Adjustment

Viable cell concentration is determined using a Bürker hemocytometer under phase-contrast microscopy to calculate exact cell encapsulation ratios.

05 MOLD

5. Cell Loading & Mold Casting

Cell pellets are gently resuspended in the GelMA bioink and dispensed into customized silicone molds or extruded via a CELLINK BIO X pneumatic bioprinter.

06 CROSSLINK

6. Photopolymerization

Constructs are exposed to controlled visible light / UV-A: free-radical crosslinking rapidly cures the hydrogel network into elastic mini-discs.

07 TRANSFER

7. Well Transfer

Solidified hydrogel constructs are delicately lifted using sterile micro-spatulas and transferred into individual wells of 24-well culture plates.

08 INCUBATE

8. Long-Term Dynamic Incubation

Constructs are cultured at 37 C, 5% CO2 inside Eppendorf CellXpert incubators, monitoring viability, morphometry, and drug sensitivity up to 30 days.
